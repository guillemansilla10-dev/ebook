# Los dos ciclos — treatment

Style: **One-line Drawing** (`styles/one-line`). 30 s, vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps. Spanish voice-over (Kokoro `ef_dora`, offline), burned-in handwritten subtitles + `.srt`. No storyboard stop (user asked to go straight to the film).

Source: the "Los dos ciclos" page of the user's 21-day ebook. Brand palette cream / plum; the style's warm paper (`#F4EFE4`) already sits on the brand cream, and the single accent is the brand plum `#7A4E5E`.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Two lines, alternating** — one line in each half of the frame, abandono left, constancia right, drawn in turns until the right one wins. Clear, but it is the ebook's two-column diagram animated; static, and it splits the 9:16 frame into two thin strips.
2. **List that turns** — the five stages of each cycle drawn as five little vignettes (a flame, a dumbbell, a battery, a weight, a stone), then the five of the other. Literal, ten vignettes in 30 s are too many to read on a phone.
3. **A loop that breaks into a spiral (chosen).** One line runs into a circle and keeps going round it — each lap slower, thinner, more tired — until it stops where it started and the ink pools. Silence. Then the same line leaves the circle at a tangent in plum and turns into a spiral that grows wider and higher with every turn until it runs off the top of the paper. The pull-back shows the whole thing at once: the closed grey ring and the plum spiral rising out of it — the two cycles as one drawing.

Chosen because the medium *is* the metaphor: a line that returns to the same point vs a line that builds on itself. No vignettes to decode, readable at thumbnail size, and the single accent colour does the "mudanza".

## 2. Logline and arc

A line that keeps going round in circles learns to grow instead.

- **Setup** (0–3 s): the pen is already running, full of energy — "Empiezas con todo."
- **Conflict** (3–12 s): it bends into a loop; each lap is slower and more ragged (effort → exhaustion → guilt), until it closes on its own start.
- **Stop** (12–14 s): the nib stops, a blot pools, the music cuts to silence.
- **Turn** (14–23 s): a tiny plum stroke leaves the ring — one minimal action — and each turn of the new spiral is a little bigger and a little higher (evidence → confidence → identity).
- **Ending** (23–30 s): pull back to the whole page; the spiral runs off the top edge and keeps going; the title writes itself in the empty paper.

Ending echoes the opening: it begins mid-line at speed and ends mid-line at speed — but the first runs into a loop, the last runs out of the frame.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Osvaldo Cavandoli, *La Linea*** — learn: one line is both world and character; a transformation of the line is the beat. Don't take: the character, its voice, its gags.
- **Norman McLaren, *Begone Dull Care*** — learn: the line moves *to* the music, note for stroke. Don't take: abstract colour fields, the jazz score.
- **Christoph Niemann's visual essays** — learn: one idea, drawn so economically it reads in a second. Don't take: any drawing.

## 6. Cue map

Solo clarinet (VSCO 2 CE `clarinet`; a different voice from the style demo's cello), 80 bpm (beat 0.75 s), D minor → D major on the turn. One unbroken melodic line; notes land on the drawing's corners.

| Section | t | Bars (4/4) | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| A arranque | 0–3 | 1 | Quick rising figure D3–A3–D4, short notes (fast line) |
| B bucle | 3–12 | 2–4 | Circular four-note ostinato D–F–E–C♯, one per quarter lap; each lap a little slower in feel (longer notes), lower velocity |
| C parada | 12–14 | — | Last note held, fades; **bus zeroed 12.2–14.0** |
| D otro ciclo | 14–23 | 5–7 | First note after silence: low D3 + tap. Rising line in D major, every spiral turn one step higher (D3 F♯3 A3 D4 F♯4) |
| E cierre | 23–30 | 8–10 | Long A4 → D5 (highest note on the spiral leaving the frame ~26.5), held to the end, fade |

Silences: 12.2–14.0 (full stop) and a near-silence 22.6–23.0 (breath before the pull-back). The first sound after the full silence (tap + D2) is the turn.

Mix: voice ~10 dB above music; music ducks ~5 dB, pen ~4 dB under voice; −14 LUFS; grain 0.

## 8. Subtitles and title

- Subtitles: Caveat, ink ~84 %, written on left to right with a paper halo, centred in the lower area (y ≈ 82 % of 1920), max two lines, numbers as digits ("21 días").
- Title: "Los dos ciclos" in Sacramento, written on in empty paper away from the drawing during the pull-back, held ≥ 4 s.
- No other text. No logo.

## 9. Compared with the style demo (after the treatment)

Demo: a life drawn as one line that reveals an old man's face. Ours differs in structure (loop → spiral, no reveal of a hidden picture), opening (mid-line at speed vs a first touch in macro), ending (the line runs off the paper vs a held face + handoff), camera path (vertical, rolling with the ring, crane up), and score (clarinet ostinato that goes round, then climbs). Shared on purpose: the pull-back as signature shot, which this style is built for.
